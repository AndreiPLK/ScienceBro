# 2026-08-29 — the moment route dies, and the lab gets an instrument

## QUESTION

Two, and the second came out of the first. (1) Is the observed hierarchy
`Delta^r log p_t < 0` for all `r >= 2` real, and on what domain exactly? (2) Does it
come from a positive-measure representation, as proposed — which would deliver
conjecture (B) as a by-product?

## NEW OBSERVATIONS

**The hierarchy is real, on a precisely stated domain.** With the whole difference
window inside the first half: 0 violations, `n = 9..32`, `r = 2..8`, decided exactly by
comparing products of rationals so that no logarithm is ever evaluated. Windows that
cross the midpoint DO fail, which is not a counterexample but the reciprocal spectrum
taking over past the midpoint.

**The limit shape was a tilted variance all along.** A prior-art check returned
Fatehi–Kittaneh Theorem 6 — the array is Poisson-binomially distributed — and with it
`f(theta) = n/sigma^2(s_t) - 1/theta - 1/(1-theta)`. The exact split
`M = n(rho beta - 1)` holds at finite `n` (0 mismatches); the limit is equality, with
the relative gap times `n` flat at 0.1890 / 0.4017 / 0.6504 across `n = 100, 200, 400`.

**`g` is the Edgeworth term, written out.**
`log rho = 1/K'' + K''''/(2K''^3) - K'''^2/K''^4` in the tilted cumulants. Tested as a
rate: with it the residual falls like `1/n^3` (column flat to ~5% over `n = 41..201`);
the Gaussian term alone leaves `O(1/n^2)`.

**Two ingredients of the main reduction turned out to be free.** `c_(J-1) > 0` at every
depth by a one-line argument, and `c_(J-3) >= 0` is not a hypothesis at all — (R)
bounds it below by a square over a positive number. Proposition 1 went from four
ingredients to two.

## COUNTEREXAMPLES

**The moment route is dead.** Any positive-measure representation forces every Hankel
matrix of `A` to be positive semidefinite. It is not: negative leading minor at order 4
forward and 5–9 reversed, at every `n` tested. Because `A` is transcendental it was
rationalised to 300 digits and the determinants evaluated exactly over `Q` — an
independent code path from the floating version — with every decisive minor clearing
its rigorous error bound by more than 200 orders of magnitude.

Also killed: general RLC preservation under self-convolution (1561 of 2800 inputs
break); harmonic additivity of `1/L` (120 of 120 the other way, in its own regime).

## STRUCTURAL CONNECTIONS

`F = G^2` means the Bernoulli sum is `Y' + Y''` with i.i.d. halves, so cumulants are
exactly twice the half's at every tilt. The prior-art check and the AP factorisation
found the same structure the same day from opposite directions.

* In the moment refutation, the first negative Hankel order **grows with `n`**

  positivity and the moment property are pulling apart, and the hierarchy sits on the
