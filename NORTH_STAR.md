# NORTH STAR — immutable mission

**Within explicitly stated consistency assumptions — causality, unitarity, analyticity,
crossing symmetry, locality, each result naming its exact subset — determine whether
consistent gravitational scattering amplitudes are forced to be string-like, and identify
the minimal assumptions required for that conclusion.**

Primary field: quantum-gravity S-matrix bootstrap.

The only accepted currencies of progress:
- new theorems;
- stronger or weaker assumptions for existing theorems;
- rigorous bounds;
- counterexamples;
- new consistent amplitude families;
- proofs that classes of theories are impossible;
- physically meaningful predictions.

We are NOT primarily building tools, auditing random papers, processing catalogues, or
reproducing other people's numbers. Infrastructure exists only in service of the list above,
under a hard budget: at most 20% of effort on infrastructure, at least 80% on physics.

Every experiment and mission carries a `north_star_relevance` field explaining exactly how
it advances the main question. Truth levels for every claim, exactly one per claim:
`IDEA → NUMERICALLY_SUPPORTED → ANALYTICALLY_PROVED → FORMALLY_VERIFIED → PHYSICALLY_REVIEWED`.
Promotion only through the four locks: independent certified numerics; adversarial
counterexample search; analytic proof with Lean verification where feasible; novelty radar
(arXiv / INSPIRE-HEP / Semantic Scholar) plus, eventually, review by a domain physicist.

This mission is never silently changed. Changing it requires an explicit founder decision
recorded in this file's git history.
