# Claim gates (binding)

States: speculative → source-supported → experimentally-supported →
independently-validated → release-ready; plus refuted / inconclusive (always allowed,
always visible).

- Promotion only through `sciencebro.schemas.allowed_claim_promotion` — deterministic.
- No claim is promoted because it sounds plausible.
- Words "discovered / proved / novel / first / confirmed / refuted" require the
  corresponding gate to have passed.
- A failed replication is not a refutation unless shown methodological or intrinsic.
- Claims are downgraded automatically when supporting evidence/validation is invalidated.
- LLM-as-judge may evaluate formatting/workflow, never the truth of a tensor equation.
- The human approves promotion to any public scientific claim.
