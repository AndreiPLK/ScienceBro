## Key extracted facts

- Pipeline validated in increasing generality: 2D local Lorentzian (three signs of λ) →
  maximally-extended Schwarzschild from invariant constraints (not supervised fitting) →
  Petrov type-I vacuum → type-I black-hole candidates with trapped interiors (p.22).
- **Author-reported residuals for a representative Schwarzschild run (FIG. 5, p.26,
  2000 points):** | |S|−1 |: min 1.66e-8 / mean 7.83e-5 / max 1.31e-2;
  |K_pred − K_Schw|: min 4.91e-6 / mean 5.05e-3 / max 1.12e-1;
  | |ρ|−1/√12 |: min 8.06e-8 / mean 9.39e-5 / max 2.33e-3.
  → Max/mean gap of 2-3 orders of magnitude: spatial failure structure exists and is
  exactly what our hidden-point maps must characterize (EV-0005).
- p.8: "the same operators are used both as a training loss and as validation
  residuals" — the paper's own validation shares the training operators. Independent
  verification (our different route) is therefore genuinely additive.
- Data Availability (p.22): scripts only; **no trained checkpoints published** (EV-0004).
- Trapped-surface check (p.21): recomputing expansions with exact fibre normals shifts
  θℓ, θn by ~5% — authors report smallness of cross terms.

## Methodology extracted (p.14-15, read 2026-08-11)

- Schwarzschild objective Eq. (45): scheduled weighted sum of L_E,Schw + Weyl + SO(3)
  + determinant-barrier terms, normalized by the sum of the schedule weights.
- Type-I Einstein loss Eq. (46): **homothety-invariant** — per-sample
  (1/(|I_θ(w_a)|+ε)) · E_μν g^μα g^νβ E_αβ, i.e. metric-contracted squared Einstein
  tensor normalized by the Weyl quadratic invariant I. NOT a raw |R_ab| norm.
  → our normalized residual (|Ricci|_F / sqrt(K)) is a DIFFERENT but comparable
  normalization; both are curvature-scale-relative. For honest comparison we should
  also compute their exact Eq. 46 quantity independently.
- Petrov profile term Eq. (47): fits S_targ(w_a) ≢ 1, quadrupolar, centred near 2 —
  explicitly repels from the Schwarzschild/type-D value.
- Post-training author validation invariants (p.14): S = 1+0i, K r⁶/m² = 48,
  ρ = −1/√12 — computed on an independent test sample but with the SAME operators
  as training (p.8).
