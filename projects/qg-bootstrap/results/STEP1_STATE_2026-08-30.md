# Step 1 of the keystone: where it stands after a full day on it

*2026-08-30. One page, so the state is readable without assembling it from eight files.
Step 1 is uniformity in the depth; the theorem itself is named in `THE_THEOREM.md`.*

## What is proved today that was not proved yesterday

**The knife is a quadratic form.** From the doubled multiset,
`SUM_t (-1)^t e_t x^t = PROD(1 - x b_k)` is a perfect square, so

    knife = g^T H g = h^T D h,   H_ij = c_{i+j},  D_ij = (-1)^{i+j} c_{i+j},

with `h_i = e_i(half spectrum) > 0`. Verified exactly, 108 settings for the first form and
36 for the second, zero mismatches (`KNIFE_IS_A_QUADRATIC_FORM.md`).

**Leg (a) is one finite difference.** The verified coefficient formula plus the classical
identity `SUM_i (-1)^i C(m,i) f(i) = (-1)^m Delta^m f(0)` gives

    c_k = den^k * Delta^{L-1} phi(0),    phi_i = tau_i / C(L-1, i),  L = J - k,

with the sign factors cancelling identically because `L = J - k`
(`LEG_A_REDUCED_TO_ONE_LEMMA.md`).

**Two exact simplifications.** The `A_r` are an arithmetic progression with difference
`2 den`; and the reciprocal identity `e_{N-k}(x) = (PROD x) e_k(1/x)` replaces a growing
elementary function by a fixed one of order `k`.

## What is measured, and how strongly

**Leg (a) itself, exactly, to depth 36.** Inside the regime `n >= 2J-3`: 15876 exact checks
over `Q(sqrt3)`, zero failures, at `J = 12..36` — double the ceiling the certificate route
reached, at seconds per depth instead of hours. It is point evaluation, not a certificate.

**The margin structure.** The awkward factor in the ratio is the central factorial ratio
`E_{t-1}/E_t`, whose decay rate is exactly the Newton excess. Both sides of the resulting
inequality tend to the same elementary limit `t/(t-1)`, so the margin is `1 + K/n` with `K`
a single constant — positive across five orders of magnitude, from about `0.066` at the
region corner to `0.2` in the limit.

## What died today, and there was a lot of it

Seven routes, each killed by an exact test, most within the hour:

1. the `tau_i` simply decrease — false;
2. `phi` factors into absolutely monotone pieces — false, the property is joint;
3. `c_t` is a positive moment sequence, giving `int PROD(1 - z b_k) d sigma` — false, 33 of
   36 settings have a negative Hankel minor (`DR-14`);
4. copositivity of the knife's Hankel matrix — false (`DR-15`);
5. generic sequence criteria (`R >= 1`, increasing, log-convex) — all three together are
   insufficient, with explicit counterexamples;
6. few negative directions in `D` — false, the inertia grows like `j/2`;
7. **Lemma A itself** — absolute monotonicity of `phi` fails at `J >= 24`, exactly.

The seventh does not hurt: Proposition 1 never needed absolute monotonicity, only the top
difference. That is the requirement that holds.

## The one structural conclusion worth carrying forward

**Gap 1 and Gap 2 are the same problem.** Four independent signs of it appeared today:

* the awkward factor of leg (a)'s ratio is the central factorial ratio `E_{t-1}/E_t`;
* its decay rate is exactly the Newton excess that Gap 2 exists to bound;
* the margin is `1 + K/n`, an effective-expansion statement of Gap 2's own species;
* the knife is a quadratic form in `h`, and `h` is the object (B) is about.

The programme should stop treating them as two problems.

## Where I was wrong, and what caught it

Three times a result came from testing outside the stated domain, and twice it would have
been reported as a refutation: the difference window past the midpoint, `k = J-3` inside
Lemma A, and points with `n < 2J-3` for leg (a). The domain is where the statement lives,
and it is not a detail.

The instrument lied three times. A thirteenth finite difference of doubles reported 75
violations that vanished at 60, 200 and 250 digits. The noise guard written in response was
itself wrong — it compared against the scale at the current order, which bounds nothing —
and passed the same noise through as "refuted". The fix is the rigorous bound
`2^m eps max|input|`, and beyond that, exact arithmetic over `Q(sqrt3)`, which is what
`lab/lemma_a_exact.py` now does. No floating point anywhere in it.
