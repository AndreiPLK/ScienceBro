# Quick judgement: Loop Quantum Gravity vs our S-matrix positivity instrument

- date: 2026-08-14
- time_box: ~15 min, web-search level (all literature readings below are `abstract_only: true` unless noted)
- north_star_relevance: maps whether LQG is a "genuine alternative" our consistency instrument can even reach today
- status: sideways-radar note, NOT a claim; nothing here is promotable evidence

## 1. Essence of LQG

Plain language: LQG says spacetime is not a smooth stage — it is *made of* discrete quanta.
Space is a network of finite "atoms of geometry" (spin networks); areas and volumes come in
smallest possible chunks near the Planck scale, and spacetime history is a foam of these
quanta (spinfoams). Main claim: you can quantize General Relativity directly, with no
background metric, no extra dimensions, no supersymmetry — gravity alone, made granular.
Technical footing: canonical quantization of GR in Ashtekar variables; kinematics gives
discrete area/volume spectra; dynamics via spinfoam vertex amplitudes (EPRL model).

## 2. Does LQG produce a graviton-graviton amplitude we could judge?

Honest answer: **no full amplitude exists**. What exists is the graviton **propagator**
(2-point function) from spinfoams: Rovelli, "Graviton propagator from background-independent
quantum gravity" (arXiv:gr-qc/0508124) computed one component at leading order in the vertex
expansion; follow-ups (e.g. arXiv:0812.5018 with the EPRL vertex; arXiv:0904.3730 second-order
terms; group-integral techniques arXiv:gr-qc/0608131) recover the correct 1/distance^2
long-range behaviour in a semiclassical limit. That is a consistency check ("does it look like
gravity far away"), not a 2→2 scattering amplitude M(s,t) over all energies. A 3-point
function exists (Rovelli–Zhang); a closed-form, all-energy 4-graviton amplitude does not.
There is no concrete testable formula our partial-wave positivity gate could ingest.

## 3. Is there a bridge to our dual-resonant machinery?

Our instrument judges meromorphic, crossing-symmetric M(s,t) with resonance towers
(Cheung–Hillman–Remmen closed-string deformations, arXiv:2408.03362) via exact partial-wave
positivity at all energies. Spinfoam amplitudes are background-independent boundary
amplitudes — a different observable, with no derived pole/residue structure in Mandelstam
variables and semiclassical control only at leading order. Verdict: **different observable,
no bridge today.** The one conceivable indirect bridge: if anyone derived LQG-corrected
Wilson coefficients for the graviton EFT with controlled errors, standard gravitational
positivity/causality bounds (e.g. arXiv:2007.15009 line of work) could test them — we found
no such published coefficients in this time box (`gap`, `NOVELTY_UNCHECKED` as a possible
future micro-question, likely POSSIBLY_KNOWN in swampland-vs-LQG discussions).

## 4. One-line verdict

LQG predicts atoms of space, not scattering formulas — it has a semiclassical graviton
propagator but no M(s,t) our positivity gate can eat; keep on radar, no actionable bridge.
