## The theorem (what is actually proved)

**Setup (p.1):** tree-level, weakly coupled, planar/color-ordered (footnote [5]) crossing
symmetric amplitude of massless colored scalars, with unknown spectrum µ(n):
dual-resonant sum A(s,t) = Σₙ R(n,t)/(µ(n)−s)  (Eq. 3).

**Assumptions, exactly:**
1. *Vanishing Regge behavior*: A ~ s^{J(t)} with J(t)<0 in some t-range → unsubtracted
   dispersion relation → s-channel-only pole sum (Eq. 2–3, p.1).
2. *Locality*: residues R(n,t) polynomial in t — finite spin at each level (p.1; nonpolynomial
   residues = infinite-extent modes, the "extremal" pathologies of numerical bootstraps).
3. *Level truncation* (THE new input, p.2): there exists a sequence ξ(k) such that at
   t=ξ(k) all levels n≥k cancel out of the sum — the Veneziano property Eq. 5 elevated to
   an axiom.
4. *Crossing symmetry* A(s,t)=A(t,s), imposed only at the special points (ξ(i),ξ(j))
   where the sum is finite (Eqs. 9–10, p.3) — then checked at generic points.
5. Gauge fixing: µ(0)=0, µ(1)=1, c(0)=1 (p.2).

**Solution route (p.3):** residues forced to R(n,t) = c(n)·Πₖ₌₁ⁿ(t−ξ(k)) (Eq. 7).
Crossing at special points solves SEQUENTIALLY as linear systems:
- i=1, j≥2 → c(n) closed form (Eq. 11)
- i=2, j≥3 → spectrum recursion µ(n) = [µ(n−1)(ξ(n+1)−1)+ξ(2)]/ξ(n+1) (Eq. 12)
- i=3, j≥4 → truncation loci recursion ξ(n) = ξ(3)(ξ(n−1)−1)/(ξ(2)−1) (Eq. 13)
Result: 3-parameter family (q,r,w), µ(n)=[n]_q (q-integers, Eq. 6/15), amplitude in
closed form via q-hypergeometric ₃φ₂ (Eq. 17); at q=1 ordinary ₃F₂ form (Eq. 18).

**Selection (p.4 + App. A):**
- Convergence of the dual resonant sum needs q≥1 (p.4, [25]).
- Unitarity kills q>1: as n→∞, a_{n,n−1} ~ −2n·a_{n,n} — leading and subleading Regge
  trajectories cannot both be positive (App. A, Eq. A4–A5, p.6). → q=1.
- Superpolynomial softness (A(s,t_k)~s^{−k} for all k) requires r=w=0 (Eq. 19, p.4).
- **Conclusion: Veneziano is the unique amplitude with level truncation + superpolynomial
  softness.** With only vanishing Regge behavior, a 3-parameter family survives
  (Veneziano, Coon, hypergeometric of [6], plus the new w-class).

**Positivity details that matter (App. A, p.5–6):**
- Partial waves via Gegenbauer in D dims; q=1 coefficients Eq. A6 — RATIONAL in
  (n, ℓ, D, r, w, µ(0)): Stirling numbers, factorials, Pochhammer. Exact arithmetic possible.
- Fig. 1: positivity-allowed (r,w) regions for µ(0) ∈ {−1.8..+1.8}, **only up to n=10, D=4**;
  region "only diminishes for D>4".
- Subtlety (p.6): positivity must be imposed ONLY above threshold µ(n)≥4µ(0) — enforcing
  it below threshold would "rule out the standard model" (Z from Higgs pair example).

## What the authors explicitly leave open (their own words, p.4–5)

O1. *Partial level truncation* λ>0 (cancel only n≥k+λ): nonlinear system "approaches the
    limits of existing numerical techniques"; suggest algebraic geometry. Linear
    perturbations around Veneziano with superpolynomial falloff counted:
    **λ=0..4 → 1, 5, 11, 18, 27 solutions**, "some or even all may be spurious… at higher
    order in perturbations"; **"we leave a full analysis… to future work."**
O2. Higher-point generalization (relocate known integer poles/zeros to sequences).
O3. **Gravity**: Virasoro–Shapiro has DOUBLE zeros in residues; "explore whether
    gravitational amplitudes are uniquely specified by their Regge behavior and some
    version of enhanced level truncation" — the direct road to the North Star.
O4. Physical motivation of level truncation itself.
O5. (Implicit) Fig. 1 stops at n=10: the n→∞ fate of the positivity region is not analysed
    (beyond the q>1 exclusion). No claim about whether the (r,w) island shrinks, stabilises,
    or collapses as n→∞.
